Case study 02 / Environmental monitoring

Environmental monitoring in cocoa supply chains

Connecting geospatial analysis with sustainability data and operations.

ContextKoa, Ghana and Switzerland
My roleGIS Analyst → Digital Innovation and Technology Manager → Head of Data and IT
Period & status2019–2023
Professional practice

Methods & focus

Earth observationGISDeforestation monitoringData systems
Koa

The question

What needed to change?

Responsible sourcing depends on reliable information about farms, landscapes and supply chains. Spatial observations become useful when they are connected to consistent records, quality checks and the reporting needs of an organization.

My contribution

What I brought to the work.

Across three successive roles, I developed geospatial analytics, satellite-based deforestation monitoring and sustainability data infrastructure. I coordinated work across technical and operational teams and supported reporting for traceability, responsible sourcing and environmental accountability.

The approach

01

Observe

Use satellite and geospatial information to examine land cover and environmental change.

02

Connect

Link spatial information to farm and operational records.

03

Check

Review data quality and consistency across records and reporting workflows.

04

Report

Translate findings into information that sustainability and operational teams can use.

Outputs

  • Geospatial monitoring and land-cover information
  • Sustainability data infrastructure and quality checks
  • Traceability and reporting support for cocoa sourcing operations

Project scope & status

The work supported operations involving more than 10,000 cocoa farmers. This is the scale of the team’s operating context, not a measured environmental impact or a claim of individual farmer onboarding.
