I'm a sustainability researcher, geospatial scientist, and planner. I'm completing a PhD in Sustainability Management at the University of Waterloo (School of Environment, Enterprise and Development), alongside a Master of Business, Entrepreneurship and Technology (MBET) — and before Waterloo I trained as a planner and GIS scientist at KNUST in Ghana.

What connects my work is a single question: **how environmental performance gets measured, governed, and turned into action** — across research, spatial data, planning, and the market. Over the past decade I've moved between those worlds: leading data and sustainability technology for a European premium food company, co-founding a geospatial analytics venture in West Africa, and publishing on conservation policy, digital innovation, and working landscapes.

What sets my work apart is that I don't stop at mapping or modelling environmental change — I connect it to the governance systems, planning processes, and markets that decide what happens next. That combination of geospatial science, governance research, and green enterprise is unusual, and it's where I do my best work.

## What I work across

- **Research & academia** — sustainability governance, digital agriculture, working landscapes, and sustainability transitions. Eight peer-reviewed publications and CAD 70,000+ in competitive funding, including the CAPI Doctoral Fellowship.
- **Geospatial & spatial data science** — Earth observation, GIS, and spatial analytics for environmental monitoring and decision-making. See my [projects](/portfolio/).
- **Planning** — land-use, environmental, and resource planning, grounded in a BSc in Planning, an MPhil in GIS, and membership in the Ghana Institute of Planners.
- **Sustainability consulting & ventures** — ESG reporting, supply-chain traceability, deforestation monitoring, and environmental-market strategy, drawn from corporate and startup practice. See [consulting](/consulting/).
